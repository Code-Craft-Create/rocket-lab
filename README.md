# Skyhop Express 🚀

A cartoon rocket-launch upgrade game in a single `index.html`, with no external assets. Every picture, sound effect and music track is generated in code (Canvas 2D + WebAudio).

Grandma Ada's backyard rocket shed starts a passenger line: fly the folks of Pebbleton to a floating café, a lightning lab, an orbital hotel, the Moon, Mars, a Saturn ring spa and finally the mysterious Starlight Gate.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or enable GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`, root folder).

**Controls**

| Action | Keyboard | Touch / mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Launch (hit the green zone!) | Space | Tap | A |
| Steer | ← → or A D | Hold left / right half of the screen (or turn on **Tilt to steer** in Settings) | Left stick or d-pad |
| Afterburner (once unlocked) | Space | BOOST button | A |
| Dash (once unlocked) | Double-tap ← or → | Double-tap the left or right half | LB / RB, or flick the stick twice |
| Pause | P / Esc | ⏸ button | Start |
| Workshop: pick an upgrade | Arrow keys | Tap it | D-pad or stick |
| Workshop: buy it | B | Buy button | X |
| Workshop: switch tabs | Q / E | Tap a tab | LB / RB |
| Close a panel / back to the workshop | Esc | ✕ button | B |

## Features

- Launch timing mini-game, then dodge birds, planes, storm clouds, jets, satellites, asteroids, UFOs, comets, space jellies and black holes.
- 34 upgrades across 6 branches (Engine, Fuel, Control, Structure, Launch Site, Cabin) that unlock progressively, with 6 either/or forks (swap any time for a partial refund).
- 10 passenger routes with a little story, passenger comfort, star ratings and reviews, plus a free-flight mode and an endless run past the Starlight Gate (keep going and someone will wave).
- Job Board side jobs (collect coins, skim obstacles, fly clean...) that scale with how you play.
- Sky forecast before each flight: sunset, night, tailwind, coin shower or ring festival, each with its own bonus.
- Surprise sky events mid-flight: gold rush, ring road, bird migration, meteor shower, ice storm, jelly bloom, and rainbows (steer up through the arc and coins are worth double for a while; hit the top for longer).
- Four sky bosses with their own music: the Storm King (dodge his lightning), Big Blue the sky whale (ride its spout), Captain Calico's sky pirates (slide into the green lane before the cannons fire) and the Space Kraken (slip between its arms).
- Ghost racer: a faded rocket replays your best run on each route.
- Passenger wishes: riders ask for a ring, a wiggle, a close call or a calm stretch mid-flight. Grant them for comfort and coins.
- Daily Special: a new delivery every day (same for everyone), with a twist like Glass Cannon, Feather Day or Boss Day, a target height and a day streak.
- Sky Guide: a field book that fills with drawings of every obstacle and boss you meet.
- Guestbook: every passenger you fly gets a portrait and a short bio.
- Lost & Found: your passengers keep dropping things (a top hat, a pancake, a bowling ball...). Catch them in the sky at the right height and return them (two of them earn paint jobs).
- A rival: Baron Vroom of Vroom Rockets Inc. turns up after story milestones and races his own rocket beside yours. Beat his height for a prize; beat all five of his rockets and he becomes your biggest fan.
- Name your rocket in the Paint Shop (or roll a random name like The Zippy Noodle). The name goes on the launch pad, the banner plane and your postcards.
- Welcome back: if you return after an hour or more, the souvenir stand has kept selling snow globes for you (up to eight hours' worth).
- Add it to your phone's home screen and it runs full screen with its own rocket icon.
- Seasons: the yard follows your calendar, with snow and roof lights in winter, blossoms and tulips in spring, sunflowers and fireflies in summer, falling leaves in autumn and carved pumpkins with bats at the end of October. Tap the seasonal friend next to Ada.
- Fan mail: thank-you letters with a little gift arrive in the yard's mailbox after deliveries, boss wins and lost-item returns.
- Scenery that changes as you climb: hills and town, the town blimp, a castle on a floating island in the High Sky, then the Moon, Mars, a ringed planet and the Gate.
- Route Map with an illustrated panorama of the whole line, from the shed to the Starlight Gate.
- A pokeable workshop: tap the rocket, Grandma Ada, the crowd or the shed.
- Paint shop with paint jobs and exhaust trails (rainbow, hearts, stardust, and four earned from the bosses), 34 achievements (two of them secret), each with its own medal, and shareable postcards of your deliveries.
- Logbook: a chart of your recent flights' heights, personal records (top speed, most coins, longest ring combo, most close calls...) and all-time totals. Beat a skill record and the results card says so.
- After the finale, open a new line (new game plus): start over with a fresh rocket, keep your trophies and cosmetics, and earn +50% per line.
- Relaxed mode (+2 hearts, fewer hazards), tilt steering on phones, music and sound volume sliders, and a calmer-screen option (less shake and flashing).
- Save codes (Settings): copy your progress from one device and load it on another.
- Dash Jets: a late Control upgrade that lets you double-tap to zip sideways with a barrel roll, untouchable mid-dash (zip straight through a hazard for a bonus and the Untouchable medal).
- Your rocket visibly changes as you upgrade: nose cones, fins, boosters, drop tanks, stages, shield bubble, ram prow and more.
- Procedural music (seven songs) and ~40 synthesized sound effects.
- Plays on desktop and phones (portrait and landscape). Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage).
